2.13 Thermal Management Ventilation System
==========================================

This project implements an intelligent cooling system that dynamically regulates fan operation speed according to environmental temperature measurements obtained from precision thermal sensors.

The automated climate control mechanism employs digital temperature monitoring to continuously assess ambient thermal conditions and proportionally adjusts ventilation motor velocity to sustain optimal environmental comfort. Elevated temperature readings trigger corresponding increases in airflow circulation rates.

How it Works?
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
.. image:: img/Fan_Code3.png

The voltage of A0 (GP26) is read and assigned to the variable [Vr].

.. image:: img/Fan_Code4.png


These calculations convert the thermistor values into centigrade degree. 


.. note::
    Here is the relation between the resistance and temperature: 

    **RT =RN expB(1/TK – 1/TN)** 

    * RT is the resistance of the NTC thermistor when the temperature is TK. 
    * RN is the resistance of the NTC thermistor under the rated temperature TN. Here, the numerical value of RN is 10k. 
    * TK is a Kelvin temperature and the unit is K. Here, the numerical value of TK is 273.15 + degree Celsius. 
    * TN is a rated Kelvin temperature; the unit is K too. Here, the numerical value of TN is 273.15+25.
    * And B(beta), the material constant of NTC thermistor, is also called heat sensitivity index with a numerical value 3950. 
    * exp is the abbreviation of exponential, and the base number e is a natural number and equals 2.7 approximately. 

    Convert this formula **TK=1/(ln(RT/RN)/B+1/TN)** to get Kelvin temperature that minus 273.15 equals degree Celsius. 

    This relation is an empirical formula. It is accurate only when the temperature and resistance are within the effective range.


.. image:: img/Fan_Code5.png

When the temperature is higher than 25 degrees Celsius, set GP14 to ON and GP15 to OFF to make the motor rotate, or you can reverse their levels. When the temperature is lower than 25 degrees Celsius, set GP14 and GP15 both low to stop the motor.
